# Why no front end

The product owner's weekly work is reviewing feedback, checking evidence, recording promises and deciding what happens next. A database and an agent can do that from imported posts and recorded account conversations.

The free version renders private read-only reports. It has no public voting portal, mobile capture, offline collaboration, notification service or drag-and-drop roadmap. Those are useful screens for other participants; they are not implemented here. Keep any needed collection channel until its replacement is tested. Enterprise DNA can build the customer portal and connections into an installed version.

Reports never send or update data. Act through the CLI with a named operator. Do not host these private reports publicly.
